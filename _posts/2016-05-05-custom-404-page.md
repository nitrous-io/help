You can add a custom &ldquo;404 Not Found&rdquo; page by adding
your own `404.html` file to the root of your project.

```html
<!-- 404.html -->
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
  <meta charset="utf-8">
  <title>Page not Found</title>
</head>
<body>
  <h1>Oh noes, page could not be found</h1>
  <p>Uh oh, we could not find the page you requested. Why don't you
  check out the following pages instead?</p>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="/exciting">An Exciting Page</a></li>
    <li><a href="/thrilling">A Thrilling Page</a></li>
    <li><a href="/arousing">An Arousing Page</a></li>
    <li><a href="/stimulating">A Stimulating Page</a></li>
    <li><a href="/dramatic">A Dramatic Page</a></li>
  </ul>
</body>
</html>
```
