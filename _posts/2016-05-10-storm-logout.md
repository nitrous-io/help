### Name

storm logout - Log out from your current PubStorm session

### Synopsis

<pre><code>storm logout</code></pre>

### Description

Use `storm logout` to logout using your [PubStorm](http://www.pubstorm.com/)
account. Use [`storm login`]({% post_url 2016-05-10-storm-login %}) to log back
into your PubStorm account.
