### Name

storm projects - List PubStorm projects you have access to

### Synopsis

<pre><code>storm projects</code></pre>

### Description

`storm projects` displays PubStorms projects that you own, as well as projects
that you are a collaborator of.

```bash
$ storm projects
Your Projects
- blog
- company-homepage
Projects Shared With You
- wedding-registry
```
